Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29/05/26

This Privacy Policy explains how DateMap (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the DateMap iOS app. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable.

1. Who we are

DateMap is a mobile app for couples to discover, save, and log date spots in London. The app is operated by DateMap.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at datemapinfo@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the DateMap iOS application and related services we provide. It does not cover third-party websites or services that you may access through links in the app; those services have their own privacy policies.

3. Data we collect and why

We collect only the data we need to run the app, keep it secure, and provide the features you use.

Account and profile

  • Email address — to create and secure your account, and to contact you about your account if needed.
  • Display name and avatar photo — to show your profile to you, your linked partner, and (if you choose) other users.
  • Sign-in information — when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive identifiers from that provider as part of authentication (we do not receive your Google or Apple password).

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (providing the app).

Couple linking and shared data

  • Partner link status — to connect your account with your partner’s account.
  • Invite codes — short-lived codes used to link accounts; we do not keep active invite codes longer than needed for linking.
  • Shared content — when you are linked, certain data is visible to both partners, including date logs, shared wishlists, shared date plans, and in-app partner notifications.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you; your choice to link with a partner.

Content you create

  • Date logs — text notes and photos (up to five photos per log) that you save about dates.
  • Location ratings — your ratings of date spots.
  • Wishlists and wishlist items — places you save to visit.
  • Date plans — itineraries you create, including spots added to a plan.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Location and maps

  • Device location (optional) — only if you grant permission, to show your position on the map and improve map-related features. You can withdraw permission in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Map usage — map tiles and related requests are handled by Mapbox; they may process technical data such as IP address and device information under their own policy.

Legal basis: your consent (device location); performance of our contract (map features).

Search and places

  • Google Places — when you search for or add places, your queries are sent to Google’s Places API so we can show suggestions and place details.

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Preferences

  • Public profile setting — whether you choose to make your profile public (see section 5).
  • Notification preferences — such as muting certain in-app notification types.

Legal basis: your consent (public profile); performance of our contract (notification settings).

Diagnostics and security

  • Error and crash reports — sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs and improve stability. We do not use this for advertising.
  • Technical logs — limited operational logs needed to secure the service and investigate abuse.

Legal basis: legitimate interests (security, reliability, and improving the app).

4. Partner linking and shared data

If you link with a partner, you both can see shared couple data as designed in the app — for example, each other’s date logs, shared wishlists, shared plans marked as shared, and in-app notifications about partner activity.

You control whether to link or unlink. If you unlink, shared wishlists and shared plan access end for the unlinked couple. Some historical couple data may remain subject to our retention and archival rules (for example, where both partners previously shared date logs), as described in section 9 and in our in-app behaviour.

5. Public profiles

You can turn on “Make my profile public” in the app’s Privacy settings. If you do, your dates, ratings, and photos may be visible to other DateMap users — but only when both you and your linked partner have public profiles enabled. If either partner keeps a private profile, your shared date content is not shown publicly to other users.

You can turn public visibility off at any time in Settings.

6. How we store and protect your data

  • Hosting: Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL database and file storage), hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Security: Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is protected by authentication and Row Level Security (RLS) policies so users can only access data they are permitted to see.
  • Access: We limit access to personal data to what is needed to operate, maintain, and secure the service.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell your personal data. We do not show advertising based on your personal data.

7. Third-party services

We use trusted providers to run specific features. They process data only as needed to provide those services, under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, storage) — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Mapbox (map rendering) — Mapbox Privacy Policy
  • Google (Sign-In, Places API) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Sentry (error monitoring) — Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple) — Apple Privacy Policy

We are responsible for how we use these services in DateMap. If you have questions about a provider’s processing, you can also contact them directly using the links above.

8. Cookies and tracking

DateMap is a native iOS app, not a website. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking for marketing.

Sentry may collect technical information about errors and crashes to help us diagnose issues. This is not used to profile you for ads. For more detail, see Sentry’s privacy policy linked above.

9. How long we keep your data

  • While your account is active — we keep your data so you can use the app.
  • When you delete your account — we delete your account and associated personal data, including date logs and photos, through our deletion process. Some copies may remain in encrypted backups or service logs for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) before they are overwritten or purged.
  • Error monitoring — data held by Sentry is retained according to Sentry’s policies and our configuration.

10. Your rights (UK and EU)

If UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data (see section 11).
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data in certain cases.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format where technically feasible.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent (for example, device location or public profile), you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or your local EU supervisory authority.

11. How to request access, correction, or deletion

Delete your account in the app

Go to Profile → More → Danger zone → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and removes your date logs, photos, and related data, then signs you out.

Contact us by email

For access, correction, portability, or deletion requests (or if you cannot use the app), email datemapinfo@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

We aim to respond within one month. In complex cases we may extend this by up to two further months, as permitted by law, and we will tell you why.

12. Children

DateMap is for users aged 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed in the EU (Ireland). Some of our service providers (for example Google, Mapbox, Sentry, or Apple) may process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or provider commitments under applicable data protection law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version with an updated effective date. If changes are significant, we may notify you in the app or by email where appropriate.
